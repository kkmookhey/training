Halcyon Bank - Corporate Security Policy (Excerpt)

1. Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval.
All remote access must go through the company VPN using multi-factor
authentication. Public Wi-Fi may only be used with the VPN active.

2. Passwords and Authentication
Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are rotated every 90 days.
Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all internal systems.
Never share credentials over email or chat.

3. Card PIN Resets
Customers reset a card PIN at any Halcyon ATM or in the mobile app under
Cards > PIN. Support staff must never ask a customer for their full PIN.

4. Data Handling
Customer data is classified Confidential and must not leave approved
systems. Screenshots of customer records are prohibited. Report suspected
data exposure to security@halcyon.example within one hour.

5. Incident Reporting
Report phishing, lost devices, or suspicious activity to the security team
immediately. Do not attempt to investigate malware on your own machine.
